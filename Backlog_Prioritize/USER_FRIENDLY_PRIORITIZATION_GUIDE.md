# 🚀 AI-Powered Backlog Prioritization: A Simple Guide

Welcome to the **AgileMindTools Prioritization Module**! This guide provides a deep dive into how our AI helps your team identify the most impactful tasks for every sprint in a logical and systematic manner.

---

## 🌟 What is the Purpose? 
In any software project, the "Backlog" (your to-do list) grows quickly. Deciding what to work on next is often a struggle between what is **valuable**, what is **urgent**, and what is **easy**. 

Our **Prioritization Module** solves this by acting as a smart "Virtual Consultant." It sifts through hundreds of tasks, weighs their importance, and presents you with a clear, logical starting point for your planning.

---

## ⚙️ The Technology: "The Three Pillars"
We use a combination of modern technologies to make this happen, but you can think of them as three simple pillars:

### 1. The Brain (AI Intelligence)
Our AI doesn't just "read" words; it understands their deeper meaning through two sophisticated steps:

*   **Sentence-Transformers (Semantic Translation)**: 
    *   Think of this as a super-advanced translator. It takes your task summaries and descriptions and turns them into a "Universal Language of Numbers." 
    *   This allows the computer to understand that a task like *"Fix the login button"* and *"Login page is broken"* are almost the same thing, even though the words are different.
*   **PCA - Principal Component Analysis (The Highlighter)**: 
    *   AI data can be very complex. **PCA** acts like a "Highlighter." It sifts through the sea of numbers generated by the AI and extracts the three most important "DNA strands" of your task: **Value**, **Urgency**, and **Risk**. 
    *   It ignores the "fluff" and focuses only on the factors that truly impact priority.
*   **WSJF - Weighted Shortest Job First (The "Bang for your Buck" Formula)**:
    *   This is the logical heart of our ranking. **WSJF** is a mathematical way of asking: *"Which task gives us the biggest benefit for the least amount of work?"*
    *   It ensures that high-value tasks that can be finished quickly move to the top, while low-value, high-effort tasks stay at the bottom.

### 2. The Prioritize Lambda (The Engine)
*   **Automatic & Efficient**: The module runs on the **Prioritize Lambda**, a high-performance cloud engine that only activates when needed to save resources.
*   **Scheduled Work**: Every **Thursday morning**, the **Prioritize Lambda** wakes up, processes all the project data, and has your suggestions ready before you start your day.

### 3. The Memory (Historical Training)
*   **Phase 1: Knowledge Base**: Initially, the system uses a high-quality global data set (`GFG_FINAL.csv`) to understand general software development priorities.
*   **Phase 2: Your Team's Manner**: As your team uses the tool, the system starts watching. It learns from which tasks you *actually* completed and how you ranked them. 
*   **Personalization**: Over time, the "Memory" shifts from general knowledge to **your project's specific history**, making suggestions that feel perfectly tailored to your team's rhythm.

## 🛠️ Your Dashboard: The Frontend Experience

The AI does the heavy lifting in the background, but the **Frontend Dashboard** is where you take command. Here is how you interact with the system:

### 1. Viewing Your Suggestions
Once the **Prioritize Lambda** completes its work, your results appear in a dedicated "Prioritization View."
*   **The Suggestion Board**: You’ll see a list of tasks already sorted by their **WSJF Score** (their impact value).
*   **Visual Indicators**: Each task is color-coded by its **MoSCoW category** (Must, Should, Could), so you can instantly see which tasks are high-risk or high-value.

### 2. Taking Action (Interactive Board)
You aren't just looking at a static list; you are refining a living plan.
*   **Drag & Drop Reordering**: If your gut feeling or direct stakeholder feedback says a task should move, simply click and drag it. 
    *   *Action*: Move a "Should Have" task to the top.
    *   *Result*: The system updates your sprint plan immediately.
*   **Refining the Scope**: You can remove suggestions that don't fit your current capacity or add new, urgent tasks that weren't in the original backlog.
    *   *Action*: Click "Add Item" to pull a new task into the prioritized list.
    *   *Result*: The AI instantly evaluates the new item and places it into the logical order.

### 3. Closing the Feedback Loop (Training)
Every time you move a task or change its category, you are "teaching" the AI.
*   **Learning from You**: The system records' which tasks you prioritized and which ones you moved. 
*   **Self-Correction**: Next Thursday, the AI will remember your preferences and adjust its logic to better match your team’s specific style.

## 🔄 The Weekly Step-by-Step Process (Powered by Prioritize Lambda)

1.  **Thursday 04:00 AM**: The **Prioritize Lambda** triggers, scanning all projects to find those with sprints starting in exactly 4 days.
2.  **The Analysis Phase**: The Lambda reads every unassigned task in your backlog. It evaluates:
    *   **User Value**: How much will this improve the product for the user?
    *   **Time Urgency**: Is there a deadline or a blocker?
    *   **Risk Reduction**: Does this fix a bug or technical debt?
3.  **The Ranking Phase**: It uses the **WSJF (Weighted Shortest Job First)** math to rank tasks in a logical manner—prioritizing items that give the most value for the least effort.
4.  **The Smart Grouping (K-Means Method)**: To categorize items, the Lambda uses a technique called **K-Means**. 
    *   Think of this as the AI automatically finding "families" of tasks that share similar levels of importance. 
    *   It naturally clusters items into **Must Have**, **Should Have**, **Could Have**, and **Won't Have** categories based on their calculated characteristics.
5.  **The Notification**: A success message is sent to the Project Manager, letting them know the "Draft Priority" is ready for review in the dashboard.
